# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Bioinformatics Series in Botswana is dedicated to providing a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone — participants,
instructors, and organizers — regardless of gender, gender identity and
expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body
size, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, or level of experience.

We are a capacity-building workshop for scientists across Africa and beyond, and
we take the diversity of our community as a strength.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, respectful, and considerate in all interactions.
- Welcome questions at every level of experience — there are no "stupid"
  questions in a training workshop.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Credit others' work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Offensive verbal or written comments related to any protected characteristic.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Sustained disruption of sessions.
- Sharing participants' data, work, or images without consent.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please contact the
organizers directly — **Dr. Wonderful T. Choga** (wtchoga@gmail.com) or a member
of the organizing committee. Reports will be handled promptly and
confidentially.

## Scope

This code applies to all workshop spaces — in-person sessions, online channels,
and shared materials — and to all workshop-related communication.
